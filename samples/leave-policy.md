# LeadSquared Leave Policy

This policy applies to all confirmed employees across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Noida
and Chennai offices. It supersedes all previous leave circulars.

## Annual Entitlement

Confirmed employees accrue leave monthly, on the first working day of each
month. Entitlements reset on 1 January.

- Casual Leave: 12 days per calendar year
- Sick Leave: 12 days per calendar year
- Earned / Privilege Leave: 18 days per calendar year
- Comp-off: accrued as earned, against approved weekend or holiday working

Employees on probation accrue Casual and Sick Leave only, at half the above
rate, and may not avail Earned Leave until confirmation.

## Carry-forward and Encashment

Earned Leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of **30 days**. Any balance
above 30 days as at 31 December lapses and is not encashed.

Casual Leave and Sick Leave do **not** carry forward and lapse at year end.

Comp-offs must be used within **90 days** of being earned. Unused comp-offs
lapse after 90 days and are **not** encashed on resignation — employees serving
notice should plan to consume them before their last working day.

Encashment of Earned Leave is permitted only at the time of exit, calculated on
basic salary, and is settled with the full and final payment.

## Bereavement Leave

Five days of paid bereavement leave are available on the death of an immediate
family member, defined as spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law.

Bereavement leave is granted over and above the annual entitlement and does not
require prior approval. Inform your manager and hrops@leadsquared.com within
three working days.

## Marriage Leave

Three days of paid marriage leave are available for the employee's own
marriage, to be taken within 30 days of the wedding date.

For the marriage of an immediate family member — a sibling or child — employees
may take **two days** of paid leave. This is a once-per-relative entitlement.
Marriage leave for extended family is not covered and should be applied for as
Casual Leave.

Marriage leave requires 15 days' advance notice so cover can be arranged.

## Sabbatical and Extended Unpaid Leave

Employees with more than three years of continuous service may apply for an
unpaid sabbatical of one to six months, for education, medical treatment, or
caregiving.

Sabbaticals require approval from the reporting manager, the function head, and
the HR Business Partner. Health insurance coverage continues during a
sabbatical; the employee bears the premium for any dependents added during the
period. Earned Leave does not accrue while on sabbatical.

## Applying for Leave

Apply through Darwinbox under Leave → Apply Leave. Planned leave should be
submitted at least three working days in advance. Sick leave may be applied
retrospectively; a medical certificate is required for absences of three or
more consecutive days.

Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days requires HRBP approval in
addition to your reporting manager.

## Leave During Notice Period

Leave during notice period is at the discretion of the reporting manager and is
generally not approved, as the notice period is intended for handover. Any
leave taken during notice **extends the last working day** by an equivalent
number of days, unless the manager explicitly waives this in writing.

Sick leave during notice, supported by a medical certificate, does not extend
the last working day.
