## Leave entitlement

**Annual leave entitlement**

| Leave type | Days / year | Carry-forward |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave | 12 | No |
| Sick Leave | 12 | No |
| Earned / Privilege Leave | 18 | Up to 30 days |
| Comp-off | As earned | 90 days |

**Also available:** maternity (26 weeks), paternity (2 weeks), bereavement (5 days), and marriage leave (3 days).

Leave accrues monthly and resets each calendar year. Ask me for your **leave balance** to see your live numbers.

## How to apply for leave

**Applying for leave** takes about a minute:

1. Open **Darwinbox → Leave → Apply Leave**
2. Pick the leave type (Casual, Sick, Earned, Comp-off)
3. Choose your dates and add a short reason
4. Submit — it goes to your reporting manager

**Good to know**
• Manager approval usually lands within **24 hours**
• Planned leave should be applied **3 working days** in advance
• Sick leave can be applied retrospectively, with a medical certificate for 3+ days

## Maternity, paternity and adoption leave

**Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the first two children (8 weeks may be taken before the due date), per the Maternity Benefit Act. A further 4 weeks unpaid extension can be requested.

**Paternity leave:** 2 weeks of paid leave, to be used within 3 months of the birth.

**Adoption leave:** 12 weeks for a child under 3 months.

**Next step:** inform HR at least **8 weeks** in advance so we can plan cover and set up your handover. Reach out to **hrops@leadsquared.com** to start the process.

## Holiday calendar

**12 public holidays** plus **2 optional/floating holidays** you can choose yourself each year.

The list is location-specific (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Noida and Chennai differ on regional festivals). See the full calendar in **Darwinbox → Leave → Holiday Calendar**, or on the intranet homepage.

**Floating holidays** must be applied for like normal leave, at least 2 days ahead.

## When salary is credited

**Payroll runs on the 25th** of each month, and salaries are credited by the **28th** (or the previous working day if the 28th is a weekend or holiday).

**If your salary hasn't arrived by the 1st:**
1. Check **Darwinbox → Payroll → Payslips** to confirm it was processed
2. Verify your bank details under **Profile → Bank Information**
3. Still nothing? Reply here and I'll raise a payroll ticket for you

## Tax and Form 16

**Form 16** is published in **Darwinbox → Payroll → Tax → Form 16** by **15 June** for the previous financial year.

**Investment declarations**
• Declare in **Payroll → Tax Declaration**
• Provisional declaration: **30 April**
• Final proof submission: **15 January** — miss this and the full tax is deducted in Feb/March

**Common 80C proofs:** PPF, ELSS, life insurance premium, home-loan principal, children's tuition.

For a specific calculation query, I can loop in the Payroll team.

## Provident fund

**Provident Fund:** 12% of your basic salary, matched by the company.

• **Check your balance:** the EPFO member portal or the UMANG app, using your **UAN** (find it on any payslip)
• **Transfer from a previous employer:** raise Form 13 on the EPFO portal — your UAN stays the same
• **Withdrawal:** allowed after 2 months of unemployment; partial withdrawal is permitted for a house, medical needs, or education

For a mismatch in contributions, I can raise a ticket with Payroll.

## Health insurance and benefits

**Group Mediclaim — ₹5,00,000 family floater**

**Covered:** you, your spouse, up to 2 children, and dependent parents
**Includes:** hospitalisation (24h+), day-care procedures, pre/post-hospitalisation (30/60 days), maternity up to ₹50,000
**Also provided:** group term life (3× annual CTC) and personal accident cover

**Cashless claims:** show your e-card at any network hospital — download it from **Darwinbox → Benefits → Insurance**.
**Reimbursement claims:** submit within **30 days** of discharge.

**Adding a dependent:** allowed within 30 days of joining, or within 30 days of marriage/childbirth.

## Work from home

**Hybrid model: 3 days in office, 2 days remote.**

• Anchor days are set by your team — check with your manager
• Apply for remote days in **Darwinbox → Attendance → WFH Request**
• **Extended remote** (2+ weeks, e.g. for a family situation) needs manager plus HRBP approval
• Fully-remote roles are approved case by case at the function-head level

Ensure a stable connection and availability during core hours (**10:00–17:00 IST**).

## Submitting an expense claim

**Submitting an expense claim**

1. **Darwinbox → Expenses → New Claim**
2. Pick the category (Travel, Meals, Internet, Client Entertainment)
3. Upload a legible receipt — GST invoices required above ₹5,000
4. Submit for manager approval

**Timelines:** claims must be filed within **45 days** of spend; approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle (**5–7 working days** after approval).

**Limits:** Internet ₹1,500/month · Meals ₹500/day on travel · Local travel at actuals with receipts.

Want the **status of an existing claim**?

## Employee referral bonus

**Employee referral programme**

• Submit via **Darwinbox → Recruitment → Refer a Candidate**
• **Payout:** ₹25,000 (junior) / ₹50,000 (senior) / ₹1,00,000 (leadership & niche roles)
• Paid in two halves — 50% when the referral joins, 50% after they complete **6 months**
• Referrals stay active for **6 months**; you can't refer for your own reporting line

Payouts appear in the next payroll cycle after the milestone.

## Appraisals and increments

**Two review cycles a year**
• **April** — full review, ratings, increments and promotions
• **October** — mid-year check-in, no rating change

**How it works:** self-assessment → manager review → calibration → HRBP moderation → letters released. Increments are effective **1 July**.

**Ratings:** Exceptional / Exceeds / Meets / Needs Improvement, assessed on goal achievement plus values.

If you disagree with a rating, you can raise it with your manager within **7 days** of the release, and then with your HRBP. Tell me if you'd like me to connect you to your HRBP.

## Resignation, notice period and exit

**Notice period:** 60 days for all confirmed employees (30 days during probation).

**Process**
1. Discuss with your manager first
2. Submit in **Darwinbox → Separation → Initiate Resignation**
3. Complete the exit checklist — knowledge transfer, asset return, clearances
4. Attend the HR exit interview

**After your last day**
• **Full & final settlement:** within 45 days
• **Relieving & experience letters:** within 7 working days of clearance
• Unused earned leave is encashed; notice shortfall is recovered from F&F

This is a significant decision — your HRBP is happy to talk it through confidentially first.

## Onboarding documents

**New joiner checklist**

**Documents:** PAN, Aadhaar, passport photo, last 3 payslips, relieving letter from your previous employer, educational certificates, and cancelled cheque.

Upload everything in **Darwinbox → Onboarding → Documents** within your first week.

**Your employee ID** is issued on day 1 with your Microsoft credentials. **Background verification** completes in 7–15 days.

Need a **letter for a bank or visa**? Request it under **Letters → Employment Verification** — issued in 2 working days.
